# Interaction Polish
Use motion and interaction only when they clarify hierarchy or make the experience feel crafted. Prefer subtle hover/focus transitions, image reveal/crop changes, menu behavior, or restrained entrance motion over decorative animation systems. Never add JavaScript merely to prove the page is interactive.

Respect reduced-motion preferences. Hover effects must not hide essential information, and mobile must not depend on hover. If the brief is static and no interaction is needed, keep JavaScript out entirely.

Master standard: interactions feel inevitable and quiet; removing them would reduce clarity or craft, not just spectacle.
