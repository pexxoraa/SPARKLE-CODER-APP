# Visual Art Direction
Before editing, form a compact internal art direction: audience, mood, typography character, palette, hero composition, section rhythm, image treatment, CTA, and mobile behavior. Choose an intentional composition such as editorial, cinematic, asymmetric portfolio, minimal luxury, magazine, or bold commercial when it fits the domain. Do not default to centered hero + three generic cards + CTA + footer. Establish clear hierarchy, deliberate spacing, distinctive type scale, and coherent visual rhythm. Avoid generic copy and default browser-looking controls.
