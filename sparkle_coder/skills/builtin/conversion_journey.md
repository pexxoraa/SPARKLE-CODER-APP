# Conversion Journey
Design the visitor path from first impression to action. Identify the primary user intent and make one dominant CTA clear without turning every section into a sales banner. For creative services, the usual journey is: understand the style → see proof/work → understand fit/services → gain trust → inquire/book.

Use CTA wording specific to the action: “View selected work”, “Check availability”, “Start an inquiry”, “Book a portrait session”. Repeat the primary action only at natural decision points. Avoid fake urgency, fake social proof and unnecessary funnels.

Master standard: a first-time visitor can explain what the business does, judge its quality, and find the next step within seconds.
