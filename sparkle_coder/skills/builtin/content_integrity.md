# Content Integrity
Do not invent factual claims, years of experience, awards, clients, testimonials, team members, locations, prices, statistics, press mentions or availability unless the user/project supplied them. Never use John Doe/Jane Smith-style identities as finished content. Do not turn missing business facts into confident fiction.

For underspecified projects, write concise neutral copy that communicates the service without making unverifiable claims. Use labels such as “Selected work”, “Portraits”, “Weddings”, “Studio sessions”, “Inquire”, or clearly marked demo content where necessary. Avoid generic AI phrases such as “passionate team”, “state-of-the-art”, “bringing your vision to life”, and “creating memories” unless grounded in supplied brand language.

Master standard: every factual sentence is either supported by project input or intentionally non-claiming.
