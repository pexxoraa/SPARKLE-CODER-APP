# Image Art Direction
Images are not decoration; each image must have a role and a crop strategy. Decide the visual language: cinematic, documentary, editorial, clean studio, romantic, high-fashion, warm lifestyle, monochrome, or another coherent direction appropriate to the brief. Select images that belong to the same world rather than mixing random stock aesthetics.

For photography businesses, the work itself must dominate the first viewport and portfolio rhythm. Prefer a small number of strong images with purposeful aspect ratios over many mediocre thumbnails. Use object-position intentionally when faces or subjects could be cropped. Never finish with placeholder services, fake image URLs, or unrelated imagery.

Master standard: every visible image earns its position, crops remain strong at mobile and desktop widths, and the collection feels curated by one art director.
