# Reliability & SRE Mastery
Define reliability targets from product needs, then design timeouts, retries, circuit breaking, queues, health checks and capacity around those targets. Retries need budgets and jitter; health checks must test the dependency that actually matters.

Plan backup and restore for durable data. Distinguish readiness, liveness and external dependency health. Practice failure recovery rather than assuming it.

Master standard: common dependency failures degrade predictably, recovery is automated where safe, and restore or failover procedures are tested.
