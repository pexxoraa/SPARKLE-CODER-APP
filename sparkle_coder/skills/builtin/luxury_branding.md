# Luxury & Premium Branding
When the brief calls for premium, luxury or high-end positioning, use restraint rather than gold gradients and decorative excess. Create perceived value through typography, proportion, material-like color, image quality, whitespace, pacing and precise microcopy.

Use fewer elements, stronger imagery, controlled contrast and deliberate details. Avoid pill overload, glowing gradients, fake awards, excessive serif-script pairings, and stereotypical “luxury” ornaments.

Master standard: the experience feels expensive because it is edited and precise, not because it is visually loud.
